Welcome to the Desert Technology website handover.

This platform brings together a modern customer-facing website and a practical business management dashboard.

From the front page, customers can quickly understand what Desert Technology offers, browse useful product categories, see featured stock, and reach the business for local assistance.

The product catalogue supports clear pricing, product condition, availability, search, and filters. This helps customers compare laptops, gaming systems, phones, desktops, point-of-sale equipment, accessories, and other technology.

The website also presents Desert Technology's wider service offering, including security, networking, point-of-sale support, vehicle services, and practical technology assistance.

Active promotions can highlight both products and services. Customers can then contact the business directly through WhatsApp, call, or email. The experience is designed to remain clear and useful on mobile devices.

Behind the public website, the dashboard gives the owner control over products, stock, categories, brands, orders, receipts, quotations, payments, customers, promotions, users, settings, notifications, follow-ups, and activity logs.

Owner, Admin, and Staff roles help separate access responsibly.
